# PROJECT_GOAL

Final goal: a stable competitive BuriburiLand Tetris AI for real human matches.

Not a one-line-clear bot. It should combine clean stacking, survival, downstack, garbage cancellation, garbage-hole access, HOLD/NEXT planning, Tetris, B2B, combo, opportunistic Spin/T-spin, and safe KO pressure.

Safe board: brief planning pause, strong decision, hard drop.
Pressure: faster decision, intelligent cancellation/downstack.
Emergency: survival first, no panic loop.

Success must be measured: simulator fidelity, elimination of previous pathological stacks, stable garbage response, consistent survival, improved attack per piece and head-to-head win rate.
